Collective foraging from simple recruitment cascades

A threshold contagion (Watts/Granovetter LTM) embedded in space, where the signal outruns the agents: a discovery recruits neighbours through a cascade on the proximity graph; fraction-normalised activation both spreads it and stifles it once crowded (blocking); the cascade rewires its own graph. One running swarm — and the elegant coincidences build live in the four panels: the poised SOC cycle, scale-free avalanches, marginal-value patch-leaving, and the ideal-free distribution (reinforcement learning with no learner).
